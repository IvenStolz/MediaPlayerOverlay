### Contains 
- A media-player-overlay (stays on top of every window)
- Can show lyrics of playing songs
- Media-player disappears when not pinned after a short moment when not hovered
- Appears when you move and hold your mouse at the screen edge (with a subtle glow when getting close)
- Can also be opened with a keyboard shortcut
- Lets you switch between different open media apps (like Spotify, browsers, etc.) via app icons on the side
- Settings menu to adjust colors, transparency, size, screen side, and timers

### How to use
1. Download and unzip the latest release.
2. Run `MediaPlayerOverlay.exe`.
3. Move your mouse to the edge of your screen and hold it there for a moment to bring up the arrow, or use the shortcut.
4. Click the album cover to show or hide the open media apps, or use the pin icon to keep the player visible.
